A set of illustrations of Stewart Smith, an Englishman in New Zealand known for smuggling, breeding and releasing invasive fish into local waterways, in response to a brief to produce a suite of illustrations which would be inserted into an editorial.

The illustrations had to be cohesive and explore different aspects of the individual's story and life.
