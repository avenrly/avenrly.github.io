The brief for this project was a hypothetical annual audio-visual experience at The College of Creative Arts at Massey University featuring musicians Warren Maxwell and Wallace. Working as a team we were tasked to design the branding and promotional items for the event. 

I worked with Holly Smith, the concept we came up after researching the two musicians was Convergence, where Warren Maxwell's music was grounded in the physical world thematically and Wallace's music was more connected to emotions and mental health. So our event was where they converged.

In this project I mainly focused on the website and posters. (The background in Wallace's poster was illustrated by Holly)
