This brief asked us to present an information narrative on a particular species of bird in Wellington and it's rising population while including some other data about the bird. The design should inform the audience about the bird’s habitat, diet, characteristics, and feature data visualisation of the populations over time.

I chose the Kākā and took a more illustrative approach.
